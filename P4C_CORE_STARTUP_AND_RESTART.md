Their configured restart policy was `no`; MariaDB was not OOM-killed. The final Compose recipe adds `restart: unless-stopped` to all three dependencies. This correction is part of the frozen r5 artifact, not an unrecorded runtime workaround. The one-shot init retains `restart: no`.

Earlier r4 receipts are retained with `_R4` names; r4 is stopped and superseded.
